🛡️ Mathematical Safeguards & Anti-Leakage Proof
1. No 168h Future Features

Physical 168h measurements are excluded from the inference feature matrices. Module B predicts 168h drift; it never observes it.

2. No 96h Features in 24h Gate

The 24h gate strictly receives 11 features. 96h sensor readings cannot contaminate the 24h early screening decisions.

3. Zero Ground-Truth Contamination

Labels (module_a_label, iddq_drift_168h_true) are stripped before sending responses to the UI.
